Delivery

Each clip is its spec, not its render

Five fields are the ones review actually changes. Everything else — captions, framing, sound, palette — is house style and lives in code, identical on every clip for every channel.

What the engine decides

One job at a time. Resolve serves its scripting API on its own GUI thread, and Remotion's asset directory is shared, so two VODs cannot render at once. The queue is the constraint stated honestly rather than a limit imposed by the interface.

The rule that shapes the cut

A clip is about one thing, titleable in one line. When the speaker pivots to another subject, that pivot is a boundary — not something to clip across. If describing it needs the words “and then he talks about”, it is two clips.
